| Intent        | Trigger                                   | What should be emphasized                                                                                         | What should be dimmed                   | What must remain unaffected |

## 2. Visual Primitives

- **Choice marks** (`Board.svelte`, `.under` and `.over`): two-tone as in every title, a dark band (`.under`) under a cyan core (`.over`, `--punct-mark`); dashed ones have each dash outlined on its own. The active player's tray keeps orange (`--punct-target`).
- **Piece ring** (`.top-ring`, inside the piece's group after the piece, outside the node that moves it): the top face's outline inset a little; `role-movable` solid cyan, `role-chosen` and `role-staged` thicker; once something is chosen the other movable pieces' rings fade (`.faded`, 40%). Inputs `session.movablePieces`, `session.chosenPiece`, `session.staged`.
- **Target ring** (`.target-ring`): a dashed ring round a hole for an empty space (drawn on the board under the pieces), or round the top dot of the piece the PÜNCT would land on (inside that piece's group, so nearer pieces cover it). Spaces under the chosen piece itself get their ring and click area above everything. Once a space is chosen they stay, dimmed to 65% (`.target-ring.faded`), all but those under the hovering piece. Inputs `session.destinations`, `session.stagedCells`.
- **Piece** (`PieceSlab.svelte`, geometry in `board/pieceShape.ts`, flecks in `board/specks.ts`): a slab on its level, its outline the dots joined by bars as wide as the piece and rounded at every outer corner; its side straight up and down like GIPF's discs, only its edges slightly rounded top and bottom (`profileAt`), drawn as slices bottom first, each the outline drawn in by the rounding at its height and shaded GIPF's way by which way it faces (gradients in `PieceDefs.svelte`, one per tone and shade level, shared per SVG); a piece on another rests a seam above it (`SEAM`, 0.12 of the thickness, `view.levelBase`), as DVONN's do, and its shadow falls on the piece below as a dark crease (`.shadow.crease`), so same-coloured levels part; black's rim catches more light than its top, and every top has a light edge (`.top` stroke) so pieces crossing pieces of their own colour part; the three dots are shallow flat-bottomed dishes like TZAAR's centre (`board/dishShape.ts`): a curved wall (a cosine) round a wide flat floor, drawn ring by ring from the lip inward, each shaded far to near by a gradient (`PieceDefs`) so the shade turns smoothly round it: the far wall in the lip's shade easing into the floor, the near wall catching the light; the PÜNCT's in its smooth insert with a soft sheen. Flecks on the top, down into the minor dishes and on the side are seeded by the piece's id, laid out on the piece unturned and turned with it. A piece keeps whichever of the six ways round it was put (the straight piece with its PÜNCT in the middle too, though half a turn round is the same placement), so its flecks never jump. A soft contact shadow of its outline lies at its base, on whatever is under it. Never carries interaction state.
- **PÜNCT marker** (`PunctMarker.svelte`): the rulebook's round PÜNCT-piece, standing where a moving piece's PÜNCT was while the move is staged.
- **Board theme** (`app.css`, `Board.svelte`): the HUCH edition's board: a plate (`BOARD_DEPTH`) of pale mint honeycomb cells (`--punct-play`) with thin dark outlines (`--punct-grid`), its edge showing where it faces you (`view.plateSides()`, shaded by the light) and a soft shadow on the table; each hole cut through it, its far wall in shade (`#punct-hole-wall`) and the base seen below it (`#punct-hole-floor`, drawn a plate's depth down and clipped to the holes); the central hexagon's 19 cells green (`--punct-centre`); the green surround as the table (`--punct-table`); white panels.
- **Coordinate labels** (`.coordinate-label`, from `board/coordinates.ts`): columns A–Q, each numbered up from 1 at its bottom space (`spaceLabel`, A1–A7 … H1–H16 … Q1–Q7), as GIPF, ZÈRTZ and LYNGK; each column labelled with its bottom space's name below it (A1) and only its top space's number above it (7), on the table in dark ink. Every label of a row stands the same distance out along its column, as far as the inner columns' labels need to clear the cells by a small gap (`LABEL_PADDING`); A and Q take that same distance rather than their own. History, the banner and aria labels use the same names; the logic package keeps BoardSpace's (`spaceName`, A2–A8) for its tests and the scenario builder. They can be hidden with the A1 toggle (`showCoordinates`); beside it the Levels and Far side toggles.
- **Hovering piece** (`animators/stagedMotion.svelte.ts`, `StagedMotion` on the animator; drawn by `Board.svelte`'s `hovering` snippet): the piece you place or move hovers a little over a piece's thickness above where it will rest, its shadow on the surface below, lighter the higher it is. Board.svelte's one `$effect` hands it `session.staged` (GSAP is imperative). A new piece falls to where it would rest, bounces off it up past the hover and settles into it; a moved piece lifts off where it stands and flies over everything into its hover (drawn over the other pieces while `aloft`); a turn swings it round its PÜNCT (`power2.inOut`), clockwise as chosen, the other way if that sweep is blocked by anything taller than the hover, or, if both are, up clear, round and back down. While turning it is ordered against the other pieces by every space it sweeps over (`hover.cells`), so it passes over lower pieces there rather than under them. Drop (and Place) brings it down with a small bounce in the action's timeline; Undo raises the dropped piece back into its hover before the session stages it again.
- **Level badges** (`.level-badge`, `Board.svelte`): with the Levels toggle on (`showLevels`, off by default), a white badge with the black level number (2 for a piece on one piece) at the near right of each raised piece's PÜNCT, as TZAAR's counts, inside the piece's group after its marks, so it sits over the selection ring while nearer pieces still cover it; it registers with the animator as one of the piece's nodes, so it moves with the piece.
- **Far side** (`session.view`, `TURNED_VIEW`): a toggle (`turned`, off by default) turns the board half round so what a pile hides comes in front of it; spaces, labels, pieces (three steps further round), draw order and motion all read the one view.
- **Winning chain** (`.win-spot`): at a game won by connection, a gold spot on each top dot of the chain.

## 4. Shared State Contracts

- **Draw order**: pieces, the staged piece and the marker are drawn in one order: over a shared space the lower first; otherwise, of their nearest spaces in neighbouring columns, the farther first.
- **Animation scope**: the board animates only `own-action`, `other-action`, `undo`, `history` and `exploration` transitions; page loads, server syncs and view switches show the board at rest. Motion is read from the two boards by piece id: another player's new piece drops in (its shadow waiting on the space and darkening), a moved one glides, a removed one lifts off. Your own drop lands the hovering piece; an undone one rises back into its hover. Each piece's shadow is its own node (`animator.pieceShadow`), so it stays down as the piece moves. Undo and history play in 0.18 s.
- **Staged selection**: `from` (a piece by its PÜNCT), `at` (the PÜNCT's space), `rotation` (each tap on the staged piece turns it to its next way of lying there, clockwise). Undo pops the highest manual stage; with none, it undoes the last action, which comes back staged as it was chosen (`beforeNewState` on `undo`). Tapping anything on the board that cannot be chosen lets go (`session.letGo()`); the chosen kind and the last rotation of a new piece stay.

## 5. Regression Checklist

- A new piece placed by you stands where it was staged and does not jump or blink as the state lands.
- Every piece shows its side and stands on its level; a piece on top is drawn after those under it.
- Target rings on top dots are covered by nearer pieces; those on empty spaces lie under every piece.
- Tapping off the choices lets go; the other movable pieces' rings fade once one is chosen.
- The board's position is the same on your turn, while waiting and in history.
