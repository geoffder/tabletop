## 2. Visual Primitives

- **Entry dot** (`Board.svelte`, `.dot.entry`, `.dot-ring`): inputs `session.entryDots`, `session.chosenDot`.
- **Choice marks** (`Board.svelte`, `.under` and `.over`): the dot rings, target rings, arrows and the movable, chosen and target stack rings are two-tone as in DVONN, YINSH and LYNGK, a dark band (`.under`) under a cyan core (`.over`, `--gp-mark`), so they read on the board and on light and dark pieces alike; dashed ones have each dash outlined on its own (shorter square-capped dark dashes), so what is under shows between dashes. Row rings and take/leave marks stay one-tone. The active player's tray keeps orange (`--gp-target`), as in the other titles.
- **Push target** (`Board.svelte`, `.target`; path from `board/marks.ts` `pushArrow`): an arrow lying on the board from the staged stack's top to near the first spot's centre (`ARROW_START`, `ARROW_REACH`), so it marks where the push goes, and a dashed ring on the first spot. Every arrow's tail is as far from its dot on screen as an upright one's, so slanted ones, which the tilt shortens less, reach as far back over the staged stack. Inputs `session.targets`. Click areas, bottom up: each first spot's (the stack there), then the stack staged on the chosen dot (nearer, so it covers them), then the arrows.
- **Move target** (`Board.svelte`): a dashed ring on an empty landing spot, or on the top face of a stack to cover; a click area the height of the stack there. Inputs `session.moveTargets`.
- **Stack ring** (`Board.svelte`, `.top-ring`, arcs from `board/marks.ts` `topRing`, on each stack's top face, after the stack and outside the node that moves it): `role-movable` solid cyan, `role-chosen` thicker cyan, `role-target` dashed cyan, `role-row` dashed red; broken where it would cover the top disc's mark (`markGaps`), dashes fitted whole to each arc. Once a stack or dot is chosen, the other movable stacks' rings and the dot rings fade (`.faded`, 40%). Inputs `session.movable`, `session.chosenFrom`, `session.chosenDot`, `session.rows`.
- **Row marks** (`.mark-spot`, `.toggle-ring`): once a row is chosen, a spot on each top face, red going, green kept; GIPF-pieces and loaded pieces (which may stay) also a ring at the trough. Inputs `session.removing`, `session.keep`, `session.rowKeepable`.
- **Piece** (`PieceShape.svelte`): each disc an `<image>` of its look, pre-rendered to `src/lib/images/looks/<tone>-<kind>-<turn>.webp` by `scripts/render-looks.mjs` from `DiscLook.svelte`, turned in 30° steps by the disc's id (`turnOf`; 42 looks), drawn at 4× and scaled down to 6.25 px per board unit (334 px across: 2.5× the largest size the board fits at, the readiness review's limit) so the fine bands of the grooves and side blend with no steps or seams (geometry in `board/pieceShape.ts`; the `/looks` dev page draws them live for the script). GIPF's lathe-drawn disc with a filleted groove, a basic piece plain (a GIPF-piece two of them, a loaded piece one under its potential), each potential with its mark (TAMSK a second groove, ZÈRTZ three half-cylinder notches across the rim, DVONN a hole cut through, YINSH a channel, PÜNCT notches cut through the edge). Drawn live, the looks made opening a game and each move slow on phones. Never carries interaction state.
- **Board theme** (`app.css`; grid lines from `board/boardLines.ts`): GIPF's board and colours: a pale blue play area (`--gp-play`) bounded by the outermost grid lines, black lines and dots, on a muted blue table (`--gp-table`, `--gp-table-deep`); dark navy panels and tabs, pale blue cards and history. No intersection marks, except an optional one on E5 (`Board.svelte`, `.station`; not on the real board): a ring just wider than a piece with one as wide as TAMSK's inner groove inside it, the grid lines running through it to the inner ring, which is filled with the play area's colour to stop them, reminding that a TAMSK-loaded piece pushed there earns an extra move. It is off by default (see Board toggles).
- **Coordinate labels** (`.coordinate-label`, from `board/coordinates.ts`): each column's bottom and top point (A1…A5 to I1…I5). They can be hidden (see Board toggles).
- **Board toggles** (`OverlayToggle.svelte`, in `GameTable.svelte`'s overlay): a row of round chips at the board's bottom right, as in DVONN and TZAAR: A1 for the coordinate labels, then, when TAMSK potentials are in the game, a two-ring icon for the E5 mark, then two turning arrows for the far side: the board seen half a turn round (`TURNED_VIEW`), so what tall stacks hide comes in front; labels follow their columns, piece marks turn half round with the board. Each is the player's own title preference (`showCoordinates`, `centreMark`, `turned`), saved for the player; coordinates are on by default, the E5 mark off. On, the glyph fills in dark; off, it is a faint outline.

## 4. Shared State Contracts

- **Animation scope**: the board animates only `own-action`, `other-action`, `undo`, `history` and `exploration` transitions; page loads, server syncs and view switches show the board at rest. Every step's motion is read from the two boards (`board/boardChange.ts`), following the known push when there is one: a push slides the line, a potential hops (a PÜNCT covering a GIPF-piece fades on landing as the covering pieces drop onto it), anything else lifts the discs that leave a stack and drops those that arrive. Undo and history play it in 0.18 s, drops included (`quickDrop`), after waiting at most 0.02 s for the step to draw. Taken back, a pushed piece slides back onto its dot: one your own Undo took back stays there staged (`returned`, then the restaged selection), unless the view passes to the other player as the Undo lands (the harness's Non-active view); any other push taken back, another player's Undo or a history or exploration step, lifts off its dot as it arrives (slide 0.14 s, lift from 0.06 s for 0.12 s), so the whole change plays in one timeline within 0.2 s. An extra move taken back also drops its TAMSK-potential onto the piece it left on E5; if the push had carried that piece along, the piece slides back to E5 as the potential drops onto it. `tests/undo-timing.spec.ts` holds every actionless step to 0.2 s. The drop of the piece staged on a dot is a local GSAP timeline (`StagedDrop`), and its lift-off a Svelte outro on a wrapper node: they explain a choice, not a committed move. A switch of view while a move plays stops it (`abandon`): the move is played to its end at once, the switched state shows unmoved, and the rest of that presentation's replay plays nothing. Turning to the far side mid-move lands the step being played at once (`interrupt`), since its motion was laid out in the old view; any later steps of the same replay play in the new view.
- **Staged selection** (`selection`: `from`, `to`, `dot`, `row`, `keep`): manual choices only; a single row that must be taken is derived, never stored. `to` holds only a GIPF-piece a PÜNCT is about to cover, waiting for one piece or two. `keep` is a stacked stage, so each tap on a piece that may stay in the chosen row is its own Undo step. The selection belongs to the player who made it (a restaged move to its mover): anyone else viewing, as the harness's Non-active view is after an Undo, sees none of it and Undo passes it by. Registered with the session's `localSelections`, which pops it on Undo and clears it whenever a new state is published; on an Undo of your move, `beforeNewState` then restages it (the dot, or the piece that moved, and the cover's target). A tap on the board that misses every enabled `role="button"` lets go of a chosen piece, dot or row of GIPF-pieces alone (`letGo`); rows that must be taken and keep toggles are left alone. While a committed move, Undo or history step plays (`updatingVisibleState`), the choices it started from still show as they fade, and taps on them do nothing.
- **Moves known to the session** (`GipfPotentialsGameSession`): a step back takes back `undoneMove`, the move on screen whose index is the new action count; a history step forward knows `historyMove`, set by `onHistoryAction`. The animator uses one to tell which dot a piece came in from, only when it turns one board into the other. `undoingMove`, set by `willUndo`, makes an Undo your own only while it is the move undone (`restagedMove`, by index); `beforeNewState` restages that move and the animator leaves it staged.
- **Stopping a move** (`GipfPotentialsGameSession.moveInterrupter`, set by `BoardAnimator`): `beforeNewState` abandons the move on `view-switch`, and `toggleTurned` interrupts it. Either plays the step's shared timeline to its end (`progress(1)` on the context's timeline, never GSAP's global one), so the move lands without waiting out its length. The animator tells an abandoned presentation's later steps by `presentingState`, and leaves the board alone once another state has been shown.
- **Board drawn during a move** (`BoardAnimator`, read by `Board.svelte`): `stacks` (the board each replayed action leaves, until the state lands), `entering` (the piece coming in from its dot) and `flier` (a potential in flight), both cleared after each step, `returned` (the piece an Undo took back onto its dot, until the state lands) and `pushedIn` (your pushed piece stays off its dot until the state lands). `stacks`, `returned` and `pushedIn` are writable `$derived`s over `session.gameState`, so publishing the new state resets them.
- **Chosen kind** (`preferredKind`): kept from turn to turn, not staged; the first kind that can come in when it cannot (never the plain piece while potentials remain, as it is listed last). Tapping the chosen dot, or the piece standing on it, again sets the next kind that can come in, in strip order, wrapping round; with one kind (or an extra move's TAMSK) it lets go of the dot instead. A plain piece staged while potentials remain says in the prompt that they will be lost (`forfeits`).
- **Target lists**: derived from the displayed state; empty when the viewer cannot act, including in History View.

## 5. Forbidden Couplings

- Do not derive legality in components; read the session lists.
- Do not store derived single choices in `selection`.

## 6. Interaction Matrix

| Intent A    | Intent B    | Can coexist? | Winner / combination rule                 |
| ----------- | ----------- | ------------ | ----------------------------------------- |
| choose_dot  | choose_from | yes          | Choosing one clears the other             |
| choose_line | shape_row   | no           | Rows are taken only while no move is open |

## 7. Regression Checklist

- Push: dot drops the chosen kind; tapping it again swaps in the next kind (the old piece lifts off, the new one drops); arrows push the line one spot.
- Potential: tapping a ringed piece shows its landings; the potential hops there. PÜNCT onto a GIPF-piece asks a single or a GIPF (straight in with one when the reserve has one), then hops and fades as they drop.
- Extra move: the TAMSK potential drops on the chosen dot; Skip takes it out of the game.
- Rows: dashed marks on every row; choosing one shows spots; tapping a GIPF-piece or loaded piece toggles keep; Take row needs a four holding a loaded piece broken; a row of GIPF-pieces alone offers Leave.
- Undo: a push returns to its dot staged, then lifts off; a potential's move hops back with its piece chosen; a PÜNCT cover comes back with the GIPF-piece it covered chosen, waiting for one piece or two.
- Another player's Undo, or a step back in history: the pushed piece slides back to its dot and lifts off in one motion; nothing stays staged.
- Switching view or turning to the far side mid-move: the move lands at once, with no stray motion.
- Tapping a dot or piece while your move plays: nothing changes.
- Tap-off: with a stack or dot chosen, a tap on blank board, a stack you can't move or a label lets go; tapping another stack or dot switches.
- History View: no marks or targets.

## 8. Change Rules

When touching interactive visuals:

1. identify the intent being changed
2. identify the primitives involved
3. verify whether shared state contracts are being widened
4. update this document if a new interaction or coupling is introduced
