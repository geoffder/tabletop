## 2. Coexistence and Precedence

The session derives what may be chosen and where (`placeableIndices`, `targets`, `shownTargets`, `canDrawFaceUp`, `canDrawFromDeck`, `canExchange`, `canExchangeChosen`); `Board.svelte`, `CardTray.svelte` and `ActionPanel.svelte` draw what those give.

| Intent A      | Intent B       | Can coexist?               | Winner / combination rule                                                                                                               |
| ------------- | -------------- | -------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| choose_card   | chosen         | yes                        | The chosen cards take the ring and the lift; the others keep their gold edge or dimming, so another can be chosen in their place        |
| chosen        | place          | yes                        | Lights and rings follow the chosen cards: one card's two countries, a joker's every country                                             |
| place         | exchange       | yes                        | Both are offered: a ring placed spends the card; Exchange swaps it for a draw                                                           |
| place         | nowhere        | no                         | Invariant: `nowhere` is the chosen cards having no target                                                                               |
| place         | end_placing    | yes, after the first piece | A ring places the second piece; Done ends placing                                                                                       |
| place         | draw           | no                         | Invariant: placing and drawing are separate machine states                                                                              |
| draw          | choose_card    | no                         | Invariant: the hand cannot be chosen from while drawing, so it carries no marks                                                         |
| discard_view  | any            | yes                        | The popover floats over the table and changes nothing else                                                                              |
| let_go        | place          | no                         | A tap on a ring places; any other tap on the board lets go                                                                              |
| waiting_line  | any choice     | no                         | Invariant: the viewer acts or waits; no choice is offered to a waiting viewer                                                           |
| scoring_beats | waiting_line   | no                         | `scoring_beats` wins: the prompt names the playing entry, then returns to the latest move                                               |
| scoring_beats | place          | no                         | Invariant: a scoring is a system action between turns; a game ended by it offers nothing                                                |
| any choice    | History View   | no                         | Every `can…` is false and the targets empty while history is viewed; the prompt shows the viewed move                                   |
| any choice    | a move playing | no                         | Lights and rings fade under the piece going down (`shownTargets` is empty while `updatingVisibleState`); taps do nothing until it lands |

## 3. Visual Primitives

- **Map** (`Board.svelte`, `board/mapData.ts` from `tmp/web-of-power/map/`): the Rio Grande board traced, in the printed colours washed a quarter toward parchment, each country deepening to its printed colour at its edges (baked into `coast.webp`), with no border lines. Roads in rough black ink with smudged edges; sea routes dash-dotted; alliance tags on worn parchment straddling the border between their two countries; each country's coat of arms on its seal.
- **Pieces** (`Piece.svelte`): a house or peg outline in the player's colour under a pre-rendered light image (`pnpm run render-pieces`). On the board they cast their whole ground shadow; on the player cards (`small`) the light stays within the outline and a small shadow sits just past the edge.
- **Cloister drawings**: every space keeps its printed cloister sketch; a house stands on it, never replaces it.
- **Target ring**: a solid cyan ring (`#22c8e8`, 4.5 wide) over a dark band (`#0d1626`, 8 wide), radius 35 round a cloister drawing's middle and 57 round a seal. It stays still while it waits: a repeating animation keeps WebKit repainting the whole board on phones.
- **Lit country**: drawn over the coast and under mountains, labels, roads and tags, so the map's lettering and roads stay readable on it; red, green and purple are held near their printed colours so pieces keep their contrast.
- **Cards** (`Card.svelte`): arms only, no names; the deck's back shows the Kardinal & König seal. Choosable: a 2px gold edge. Chosen: lifted 11px, 4px gold ring, deeper shadow. Dimmed: 45%.
- **Tray** (`CardTray.svelte`): the viewer's hand fanned (8° apart, each over 45% of the one to its left), a divider, the market, then the deck and discard in a recess, each group captioned ("Market", "Deck", "Discard") on one line. Card width is the tray's `--wop-card-width`.
- **Player cards** (`PlayerCard.svelte`): name band in the player's colour, the starting player's black dot (the game's start marker), "Playing" or "Winner"; points, houses left and pegs left. No card count: hands refill to three every turn.
- **Action panel height** (`ActionPanel.svelte`, `components/holdTallest.ts`): one row, given two lines' room wherever a usual line would wrap (container queries on the panel's width), and holding the tallest height it has reached at its width, so the board never moves as prompts change. A scoring line that will not fit puts its heading on its own line and the scores beneath. The ending shows inside the panel: the winner (20px) over every score (15px, 13px on phones).

## 4. Render Ownership

| Layer                                                     | Owns                                                                                                                          | Allowed Inputs                                       | Must Not Own                |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| `WebOfPowerGameSession`                                   | The staged selection (card, joker); what may be chosen and where; every action                                                | Game state, valid action types                       | Styling                     |
| `Board.svelte`                                            | Lit countries, target rings, hit targets, the let-go tap; draws the pieces and scoring beats the animators give               | Session targets, `PieceAnimator`, `ScoringAnimator`  | Legality                    |
| `CardTray.svelte`                                         | The hand, market, piles, captions and discard popover; marks from the session                                                 | Session lists, `CardAnimator.table`                  | Legality                    |
| `PieceAnimator` (`animators/pieceAnimator.svelte.ts`)     | Houses and pegs as drawn while a move plays, and their motion; pieces left per player                                         | Transition kind, `from`/`to`, the action             | Interaction state           |
| `CardAnimator` (`animators/cardAnimator.svelte.ts`)       | The cards as the tray draws them while a move plays; cards in the air (`fliers`, drawn by `GameTable.svelte` over everything) | Transition kind, `from`/`to`, the action, the viewer | Interaction state           |
| `ScoringAnimator` (`animators/scoringAnimator.svelte.ts`) | A scoring's beats, the points as counted, the prompt's heading and entry                                                      | The scoring action's metadata                        | Pieces, cards               |
| `ActionPanel.svelte`                                      | The prompt and its buttons; the waiting line; the scoring heading; the ending                                                 | Session state, history entries, `ScoringAnimator`    | Board marks                 |
| `PlayerCard.svelte`                                       | Points and pieces left                                                                                                        | `ScoringAnimator.scores`, `PieceAnimator` counts     | Anything it would recompute |

The three animators are made in `GameTable.svelte` and shared by context (`animators/cardAnimatorContext.ts`), since the board, tray and player cards each draw part of what they move.

Draw order inside the board, which taps depend on: sea, routes, land, countries, coast, lit countries, mountains and labels, roads, tags, seals with their pegs and advisor rings, cloister drawings, houses (back to front), scoring beats, then cloister rings and their hit circles last, so no nearer house covers a ring or swallows its tap. The board carries no filters or masks (`docs/phone-rendering.md`); lit countries clip to the country's outline.

## 5. Shared Visual State

- **Staged selection** (`WebOfPowerGameSession.selection`, read through `chosenCards`, `chosenIndices`):
    - Meaning: the card chosen to pay with, and a second of its colour as a joker.
    - Producer: `chooseCard`, `letGo`. Consumers: `CardTray.svelte` (chosen cards), `Board.svelte` (through `targets`), `ActionPanel.svelte` (prompt, Exchange).
    - Lifetime: registered with `localSelections`, so a presented transition clears it; Undo pops the joker, then the card. Validity: it belongs to the player who made it (`selectionOwner`); any other viewer, as the harness's protected view can be after an Undo, sees none of it.
- **Targets** (`targets`, `shownTargets`, `cloisterTargets`, `advisorTargets`):
    - Meaning: where the chosen cards can put a piece now.
    - Lifetime and validity: derived from the shown state and the selection; `shownTargets` is empty while `updatingVisibleState`, so lights and rings fade (0.18 s) as a piece goes down while the prompt still reads as chosen.
- **What may be chosen** (`placeableIndices`, `canChooseCards`, `canExchange`, `canDrawFaceUp`, `canDrawFromDeck`): derived, false or empty in History View and while it is not the viewer's turn.
- **Table as drawn** (`CardAnimator.table`, `fliers`; `PieceAnimator.cloisters`, `advisors`, `ringSizes`; `ScoringAnimator.scores`, `beats`, `heading`, `label`):
    - Meaning: what the tray, board and player cards draw, which lags the published state while a move plays.
    - Lifetime: each animated step sets it to the state the step leaves and lands it on the arriving state (`afterAnimations`); the landing writes only while the state it began on is still shown, so a switch of view is never covered.
- **Stopping a move** (`moveInterrupters`): `beforeNewState` abandons every animator's move on `view-switch`; each, playing or between steps, records the abandoned presentation and skips its later steps by `presentingState`.
- **History steps** (`shouldAutoStepAction`): a step takes a move with the system actions after it, so a turn's closing refill (and a scoring) goes with the move that caused it, and a turn's replay from History ends with its own refill.

### Motion

- **Pieces**: a house or peg drops from 38 units up, opaque within the first quarter, falling in 0.26 s, bouncing 4 units and settling in 0.14 s; pegs already on the seal slide to their new spots (0.3 s). Undo lifts the piece off, seen rising 30 units before it fades.
- **The viewer's cards**: cards fly between the hand, market, deck and discard in 0.45 s (`power2.inOut`), each after the first 0.07 s behind. A card drawn from the deck turns over in the air. The fan re-spreads as cards leave or arrive; a card landing in it turns to lie as the fan does.
- **Other players' cards**: never followed to their player card. A card they play drops onto its pile like a piece, from 1.2 card widths up and 15% larger, slowing as it lands with no bounce, shown over the first 30% of the 0.3 s fall. A card they take lifts off the same way in reverse, gathering speed, gone over the last 30%.
- **Refill**: cards fly from the deck to the market's gaps, turning over on the way.
- **Reshuffle**: at the interim scoring the discard pile flies to the deck after the scoring's beats (`finalTimeline`).
- **Scoring**: each entry about 1 s (lights 0.24 s, chips 0.3 s, count 0.4 s, hold 1 s, fade 0.3 s, the next starting as it fades); a chain draws road by road (0.2 s each) and holds 0.8 s. Only a step with the scoring action plays it; Undo and state-only history land it at once.
- **Steps without an action** (Undo, state-only history, exploration): every motion done within 0.19 s (a piece starts 0.01 s in and moves for 0.18 s).
- **Slow frames**: while a piece drops, a frame longer than 100 ms advances GSAP by only 33 ms (put back as the move lands), so a stalling phone shows the drop late rather than skipping it.
